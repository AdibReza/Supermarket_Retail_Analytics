# Supermarket_Retail_Analytics

This project is a retail analytics dashboard designed to support strategic decision-making for experiential retail opportunities within a supermarket (Woolworths-style case study). It combines sales data with external retail trends to understand customer behavior, category performance, and market context.

**Objective**

To evaluate how product categories, customer segments, and store locations perform in terms of revenue, customer spending, and satisfaction, and identify opportunities for improving in-store customer experience.

**Key Questions Answered**

-Which product categories generate the highest revenue?

-How do customer spending and satisfaction vary across categories?

-Are spending and satisfaction strongly aligned?

-How do different customer segments perform?

-How does customer experience vary across locations?

-What does broader Australian retail (online growth) indicate for physical stores?

**Data Sources**

Supermarket Sales Dataset (Kaggle) – transactions, categories, customer segments, ratings
Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) – online retail turnover trends for market context

**Key Insights**

Food & Beverages is the top-performing category across revenue and satisfaction.
Higher spending does not always mean higher satisfaction (notable mismatch in some categories).
Customer experience varies significantly across locations.
Online retail is steadily increasing, highlighting pressure on physical retail strategy.

**Dashboard Design**

The dashboard is structured for executive decision-making:

KPI summary (Revenue, Avg Spend, Avg Rating)
Category performance ranking
Spend vs satisfaction analysis (investment opportunity map)
Customer segment comparison
Location-based experience heatmap
External retail trend context (ABS data)
